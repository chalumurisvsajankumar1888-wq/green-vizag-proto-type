About the initiative

A cleaner city is a shared responsibility.

Green Vizag is a student-project concept designed to make local waste reporting simple and transparent.

🌍

Our goal

Connect citizens and administrators through a simple reporting and tracking experience that encourages responsible waste management.

🤝

Community first

Give residents a convenient way to highlight garbage issues and see how reported complaints progress.

💡

Built as a prototype

This version uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript with browser storage. It is not connected to a municipal authority.
